Biography

Dr. Tang is an assistant professor of School of Cyber Science and Engineering at Zhengzhou University (ZZU). He received his Ph.D. degree in School of Information and Engineering from Zhengzhou University (ZZU) in 2022, supervised by Prof. Shouyi Yang. From 2020 to 2021, he was a Visiting Scholar with the Queen Mary University of London (QMUL), London, U.K, supervised by Prof. Yuanwei Liu.

Research

Dr. Tang's research interests include 5G/6G networks, near-field communications, physical layer security and Cyber resilience.

主要研究方向：无线通信、物理层安全和网络韧性。
当前研究课题：

  • 无线通信物理层安全
  • 网络韧性测评

Hiring

Dr. Tang is looking for new graduate students (M.S. students) and undergraduate interns to work on exciting research projects!

  • Candidates are expected to be highly motivated and resilient against pressure. Also, they should have a solid background in computer science, communication engineering, or mathematics.
  • Graduate students will have access to research-related software and hardware resources. Attendance at international academic conferences is highly encouraged and fully sponsored. Twice the monthly stipend stipulated by ZZU is provided per month.
  • Top-notch undergraduate students with rich system implementation experience and/or strong mathematical skills are preferred.

Contact him if you are interested.
